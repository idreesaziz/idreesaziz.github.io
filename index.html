2026LLM evaluation

No One Gets an A: Distributional Bias in LLM Text Scoring

Idrees Mustafa Aziz · First author

Research study · First author · 2026

Designed a study of 11 LLMs and ~99,000 text assessments to test whether 0-10 writing scores reflected deliberate reductions in text quality.

Compared five scoring corrections. Probability-weighted scoring increased correct rankings of better versus degraded text from 51.8% to 64.9% across nine models.

2026Learning from crowd beliefs

Recovering Pre-Event Crowd Beliefs in Prediction Markets with a Risk-Calibrated Mixture of Inverse Experts

Idrees Mustafa Aziz · Sole author

7th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2026)

Accepted after peer review; selected for oral presentation · Not yet published; forthcoming in ICAIF 2026 proceedings

Built a method to estimate what a crowd believed before an event, separating that belief from distortions caused by trading behaviour. Combined models of trader behaviour according to their estimated errors.

Reduced worst-case regret across unseen market mechanisms by about 80% (from 8.8% to 1.8%) relative to direct black-box prediction.

On 1,500 simulated markets from ten types absent from training, recovered beliefs with 12.8% less error than averaging prices after each trader's first trade.

Across 98 real markets, recovered beliefs with 10.2% less error (RMSE) than averaging the prices after each trader's first trade. Tested against pre-trading surveys from projects excluded from training.

2026Benchmark design

FinEntropyBench: What Does Entropy Tell Us About Market Efficiency?

Idrees Mustafa Aziz · Sole author

7th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2026)

Accepted after peer review; selected for oral presentation · Not yet published; forthcoming in ICAIF 2026 proceedings

Built a benchmark to test whether randomness in price changes helps predict future returns. Compared neural-network, linear and tree-based models across 16 market series.

Ran 113 tests across individual markets and years, selecting models on earlier data and measuring their forecasts and trading returns in the following year.

2026Synthetic data & transfer learning

Market Decompression: Learning Latent-State Inverse Maps for Cold-Start Financial Prediction

Idrees Mustafa Aziz · Sole author

7th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2026)

Accepted after peer review · Not yet published; forthcoming in ICAIF 2026 proceedings

Trained a Transformer on simulated markets to learn patterns hidden behind observed prices, then adapted it to forecast price variability with five real training examples.

Used a Gaussian-process model to choose simulations likely to improve real forecasts. Compared identical models trained with and without those extra labels.

Against this control, reduced volatility forecast error (MSE) by 4.7% to 7.0% in three of five markets.

2026Policy-rate forecasting

A Gaussian Recency Score for Detecting and Directing Central-Bank Policy-Rate Changes

Idrees Mustafa Aziz · Sole author

2nd International Online Conference on Risk and Financial Management (IOCRF 2026)

Accepted; selected for oral presentation · November 2026

Developed models that weight recent rate changes more heavily to predict whether central banks will cut, hold or raise rates, using past policy decisions alone.

Evaluated an exploratory pipeline on 2,490 post-hold forecasts across 37 economies, achieving 0.614 macro-F1 for cut/hold/hike prediction.
